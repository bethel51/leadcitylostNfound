Lead City University Security Directorate • Official Property Recovery Guidelines

Institutional Custody & Safety Network

All items recovered across Lead City University campus are logged, barcoded, and held in secure lockboxes at designated Security Stations under permanent officer custody.

📍 Senate Security Post A

Main Command & Central Intake Vault. Holds electronic devices, passports, national IDs, and laptops.

Open 24/7 Ext. 201
📍 University Library Desk

Ground floor reception lost & found desk. Holds textbooks, study materials, notebooks, calculators, and chargers.

8:00 AM – 8:00 PM Ext. 114
📍 Student Affairs Hub

Opposite University Cafeteria. Official verification desk for student ID cards, keys, and personal accessories.

8:30 AM – 5:00 PM Ext. 305
📍 Sports & Hostels Checkpoint

Gate Guard House at Hostels Alpha/Omega. Safe intake bin for sporting gear, apparel, and gym bags.

Open 24/7 Gate Guard 2
1
Instant System Intake

When an item is found anywhere on campus, it is turned in to the nearest Security Desk or reported on LCU FindMe. Security records its intake bin tag.

2
Automated Cross-Match Notification

Our token-based search algorithm scans active student lost reports. If category, title, and keywords match, an automated email and in-portal alert is dispatched.

3
Claim Submission & Officer Verification

The owner submits an ownership claim providing specific confidential identifiers (passcode, IMEI, markings, receipt). Duty officers cross-verify before approval.

4
Digital Slip & Certified Handover

Upon verification approval, an official QR-coded Handover Slip is generated. The student collects the item with physical student ID and signs the permanent custody ledger.

📱 Phones, Laptops & Tablets: Demonstrate unlock passcode in person, show matching iCloud/Google account, or provide serial/IMEI matching purchase receipt or carrier box.
💳 Wallets, ATM Cards & IDs: Name must match student portal credentials and government/school ID. Physical presentation of matching LCU Student ID card is mandatory.
🔑 Keys, Bags & Accessories: Accurately describe key count, distinctive keychains, internal bag contents, brands, or unique blemishes before physical inspection.
  • 90-Day Retention Schedule: Unclaimed items are securely held in custody for 90 days following institutional protocol before student body donation or auction.
  • Zero Unauthorized Release: Security personnel are strictly prohibited from handing over any item without an approved digital claim record.
  • Permanent Chain-of-Custody Ledger: Every handover generates an immutable ledger timestamp containing officer name, claimant matriculation number, and proof notes.
